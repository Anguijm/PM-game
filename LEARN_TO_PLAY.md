# Learn to Play — Drydock Masters

## What is Drydock Masters?

A semi-cooperative board game where 2-6 players run a naval shipyard. You're Shipyard Superintendents — fix the fleet before the Admiral shuts you down. Work together to keep the yard running, but only one of you gets promoted.

## Your Goal

**Team goal:** Keep Shipyard Integrity (SI) above zero. If it hits zero, everyone's fired. Everyone loses.

**Individual goal:** Earn the most Prestige Points (PP). If the team survives, the top scorer gets promoted to Dock Master.

**Game end:** The game lasts up to 12 rounds. If all contract quotas are met by Round 10, the game ends early (on-time completion). If not, you get until Round 12 — but SI penalties increase in Rounds 11-12. If SI hits zero at any point, the game ends immediately and everyone loses.

## Setup

**Shared setup:**
- Set Shipyard Integrity (SI): **22** (2 players), **36** (3 players), **40** (4+ players)
- Fill the CFR bag with tokens (17 Clear, 3 Growth Work)
- Reveal the Work Order Market (player count + 2 cards)
- Reveal 4 Foreman cards
- Reveal 1 Admiral's Mandate (applies all game)

**Each player gets a Drydock Board with 4 work slots, then:**
- Choose a Ship Contract (Side A = standard, Side B = complex). Your contract determines your starting dice colors and milestone quotas.
- Take your 5 Labor Dice (colors match your contract)
- Receive $5 Funding and 3 Material
- Get 3 BAWP cards (staged automatically in slots 1-3) and draw 3 cards for your hand (Available Work Package)

**First player** is chosen randomly. Turn order rotates clockwise each round.

## The Round

Each round has 3 phases. Repeat for 12 rounds or until the game ends.

### Phase 1: Planning

An **Event** is drawn and resolved — could be good news, could be bad. Some events are persistent and drain SI every round until someone clears them.

Then each player **drafts one card** from the Work Order Market, adding it to their hand.

### Phase 2: Action

Players take turns in order. On your turn, you get **2 actions** — spend them however you like. When you're done (or out of actions), you **pass** to end your turn. After you pass, the next player takes their turn. Once every player has passed, the phase ends and Resolution begins. You can also use free actions (like trading) even after your costed actions are spent, as long as you haven't passed yet.

**Actions (cost 1 action each):**

| Action | Cost | What it does |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Stage Work** | Material (varies by card) | Move a card from your hand to an empty drydock slot |
| **Assign Labor** | $1 wage ($3 if cross-training) | Place a die on a staged card's labor requirement. Sets the countdown. |
| **Procurement** | — | Gain $3 Funding OR 2 Material |
| **Coordinate** | — | Loan one of your dice to another player |
| **Analyze Market** | $1 | Discard and refresh the Work Order Market |
| **Hire Foreman** | $10 | Take a Foreman card (passive ability) + gain 3 PP |
| **Clear Obstruction** | Varies | Remove a persistent problem. Earns +3 PP and restores +2 SI |

**Free actions (don't cost an action):**
- **Trade** — swap Funding or Material with another player

**Team actions (cost SI, once per round):**
- **Emergency Overtime** — spend 3 SI to speed up one die by 1
- **Expedited Shipping** — spend 2 SI to gain 2 Material

### Phase 3: Resolution

Everything happens automatically:

1. **Dice count down** — all assigned dice lose 1 from their value
2. **Jobs complete** — when ALL dice on a card reach zero, the job is done. All dice return to your pool (available to assign again next round). Draw from the CFR bag:
   - **Clear** (85% chance) — job passes inspection. Earn the PP on the card.
   - **Growth Work** (15% chance) — Condition Found Report! Complications discovered. You lose 1 PP, but receive +2 PP compensation and $2 (net +1 PP). The team loses 1 SI. A new repair card is staged that you must deal with. Return drawn tokens to the bag after each draw.
3. **Persistent problems drain SI** — each active problem costs 1 SI
4. **Milestone check** (Rounds 6, 8, 10) — did you meet your contract's job quota? If not, the team loses SI.
5. **Round advances** — next round begins

## Key Concepts

### Labor Dice

Your 5 dice are countdown timers. Each die has a color (Red/Hull, Blue/Engineering, Yellow/Electrical, Green/Combat, Gray/Support-Wild). Assign a die to a work order's matching color requirement. The die starts at the required time value and counts down by 1 each round. When it hits zero, that requirement is fulfilled.

Gray dice are wild — they match any color with no extra cost. Using a non-matching colored die costs $2 extra (cross-training).

### Condition Found Reports (CFR)

When a job completes, you draw from the CFR bag. Think of it as a quality inspection. Most of the time you're clear. But 15% of the time, inspectors find additional problems that need fixing — Growth Work. It's the risk you take every time a job finishes.

### Shipyard Integrity (SI)

The shared health bar. Persistent problems drain it. Missed milestones drain it. Growth Work drains it. If it hits zero, game over for everyone.

SI thresholds create escalating pressure:
- **Below 10:** Utilization bonus disabled
- **Below 6:** Procurement yields reduced
- **Below 3:** Cross-training disabled

### Prestige Points (PP)

Your individual score. Earn PP by completing work orders, clearing obstructions, hiring foremen, and meeting milestones. Lose PP from missed milestones. Growth Work costs you 1 PP but compensates you +2 PP (net gain of 1 PP, plus $2) — though the team still loses 1 SI.

At game end, bonus PP from leftover resources: 1 PP per $5 Funding, 1 PP per 3 Material.

The player with the most PP wins and gets promoted to Dock Master. Ties broken by most completed jobs.

### Persistent Problems

Some events create ongoing problems (Hurricane Warning, Dock Flooding, Congressional Audit). Each one drains 1 SI per round until someone spends an action to clear it. Clearing is expensive but awards +3 PP and +2 SI — making it one of the most valuable actions in the game.

## Tips for Your First Game

1. **Don't ignore persistent problems.** They compound fast. Two uncleared problems drain 2 SI per round — that's 24 SI over 12 rounds. The yard will sink.

2. **Clear obstructions for the PP.** +3 PP for clearing a problem is often worth more than completing a cheap work order. And you restore SI.

3. **Watch the milestones.** Check your contract — do you need 2 jobs by Round 6? Plan backwards. Staging a card and assigning a 3-round die on Round 4 means it finishes on Round 7. Too late.

4. **Trade when you're lopsided.** If you're swimming in Material but broke, trade with someone who has the opposite problem. It costs nothing (free action).

5. **The semi-cooperative tension IS the game.** You'll face moments where you could stage a high-PP job for yourself or clear a problem that saves the yard. There's no right answer. That's what makes it interesting.
